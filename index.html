UZRA, TOR JONNO26 OCTOBER, 2026

Uzra,

Jonmodiner shubhechha to shobai dibe, but ami chai amar shubhechhata tor kache ektu alada hok. Karon tui to ar shobar moto keu na amar kache.

Asole toke pawar por theke amar majhe majhe mone hoy, Allah amake amar jiboner shobcheye shundor ekta manush diyeche. Amar chokhe tui-i amar Miss Universe, ar toke ami koto ta bhalobashi sheta hoyto shob shomoy bujhaiteo pari na, Uzra. ❤️

Jaihok, ajker ei special dine tor jonno mon theke doa kori, tor mukher ei shundor hashi ta jeno shob shomoy thake. Jibone joto kosto ba kharap shomoy ashuk na keno, shob kichur majheo jeno tui bhalo thakte parish. Ar kokhono kono kichu niye khub beshi upset hoye gele mone rakhis, ami achi tor shathe. Shob shomoy tor pashe thakte chai, toke agle rakhte chai, ar nijer shadhomoto shob dik diye protect korte chai, inshaAllah. ☺️

Happy Birthday, My Uzraa. Tor jibon ta jeno tor motoi shundor hoy. ❤️
